# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sideglass is for Windows users who want a polished secondary-monitor dashboard that stays open while they work. They use it to glance at time, weather, agenda, hardware status, notes, YouTube, and AI shortcuts without leaving their main workflow.

## Product Purpose

Sideglass turns a spare monitor or vertical screen into a compact, customizable control surface. Success means the app feels native, calm, premium, and useful: widgets fit inside the available window, user layouts stay saved, and the interface never becomes a long scrolling webpage.

## Brand Personality

Modern, precise, restrained. The app should feel close to a premium Apple-style utility adapted to Windows: glassy, quiet, highly legible, and carefully spaced.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-looking dashboards, cheap card piles, oversized empty panels, broken responsive behavior, overlapping controls, page scroll inside the app, translated quote dumps, and decorative UI that makes the tool feel less trustworthy.

## Design Principles

- Fit the screen first: the dashboard must respect the current window and avoid global vertical scroll.
- Let user sizing matter: widget contents should respond to the card size, not just leave empty space.
- Keep controls predictable: drag handles, close buttons, resize corners, inputs, and buttons need clear separation and consistent behavior.
- Prioritize useful data: hardware cards should show actionable metrics such as CPU, RAM, GPU, disk usage, and temperatures where available.
- Keep the dock untouched unless explicitly requested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast in light and dark modes, keyboard-visible controls, clear labels for icon buttons, reduced-motion-friendly transitions, and text that stays readable in compact vertical layouts.
